Habakkuk Management LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our property management services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Mailing or property address
  • Information you provide through contact and inquiry forms

Property and Tenant Information

Depending on the services requested or provided, we may collect information related to rental inquiries, applications, tenancy, property ownership, maintenance requests, and communications.

Technical Information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Website usage and browsing activity

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Respond to questions and service inquiries.
  • Communicate with property owners, tenants, and prospective tenants.
  • Process property management requests and applications.
  • Coordinate property maintenance and related services.
  • Provide updates regarding properties and management services.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Maintain business records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

Habakkuk Management LLC does not sell personal information for monetary consideration.

We may share information when reasonably necessary with:

  • Property owners and authorized representatives.
  • Maintenance professionals and service providers.
  • Technology, website hosting, and communication providers.
  • Professional advisors, including legal and accounting professionals.
  • Government authorities or other parties when required by law.

We share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose, subject to applicable law.

4. Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand visitor activity, and enhance the browsing experience.

You can adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies. Some website features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

If analytics or advertising tools are used, their respective providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

5. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use external services for communication, forms, scheduling, hosting, or other business functions.

These third parties may have their own privacy policies and information-handling practices. Habakkuk Management LLC is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services that we do not operate.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party services they use.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, provide our services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances in which it was collected.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Opt out of certain marketing communications.

To submit a privacy-related request, contact us using the information provided below. We may need to verify your identity before processing certain requests.

9. Email and Text Message Communications

If you provide your email address or phone number, we may use it to respond to inquiries, communicate about property management services, or provide relevant updates.

If we offer promotional text messages, we will obtain any consent required by applicable law. Consent to receive marketing messages is not a condition of purchasing or receiving services.

You may opt out of marketing text messages by replying STOP where supported. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary.

We do not sell mobile phone numbers or share SMS consent information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13 through our website.

If we learn that we have collected such information without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, website features, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.
